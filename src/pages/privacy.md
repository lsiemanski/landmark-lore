Landmark Lore is a personal, non-commercial portfolio project that lets you upload a
photo, identifies the landmark, artwork, or subject in it using an AI service, and
saves it to a private archive. This policy explains what personal data the app
collects, why, and the rights you have over it.

This service is operated by an individual ("we", "us") as a demonstration project.
For any privacy request, contact **l.siemanski@gmail.com**.

## Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is the individual operating Landmark Lore, reachable at
**l.siemanski@gmail.com**. Because this is a small personal project, requests are
handled manually and may take a few days.

## What we collect

- **Account data** — the email address and password you register with. Passwords are
  never stored in plain text; they are hashed and managed by our authentication
  provider (Supabase).
- **Photos you upload** — the image files themselves, stored in private per-user
  storage that no other user can access.
- **Identification results** — the subject name and description returned for each
  photo, plus the folders you organise them into.
- **Limited usage counts** — a per-day count of identification and follow-up requests,
  used only to enforce fair-use rate limits.

We do **not** use analytics or advertising trackers, and we do not sell or share your
data for marketing.

## Photos may contain other people

A photo you upload can contain identifiable people (for example, passers-by or people
you travelled with). When you upload such a photo, it is sent to our AI provider for
identification. You are responsible for only uploading photos you have the right to
upload. Please do not upload photos of other people where you have no lawful basis to
do so, and do not upload sensitive or intimate images.

## How your photos are processed by AI

To identify a subject, the photo and your follow-up questions are sent to:

- **OpenRouter**, which routes the request to
- **Google (Gemini model)**, which performs the visual identification.

This processing may take place on servers outside the European Economic Area
(typically the United States). Transfers are covered by the relevant provider data
processing terms and Standard Contractual Clauses. We do not permit your images to be
used to train third-party AI models.

## Why we process your data (legal basis)

- To **provide the service** you signed up for — creating your account, storing your
  photos, and returning identifications (performance of a contract / your request).
- To **keep the service working fairly and securely** — rate limiting and abuse
  prevention (our legitimate interest).

## Where your data is stored

- Account, database, and photo storage: **Supabase**.
- Application hosting: **Cloudflare Workers**.

These providers act as our processors and host data on our behalf.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete a photo it is
removed from storage. When you delete your account, your photos, identification
results, folders, and login are deleted in a single cascading operation and are not
retained.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you.
- **Rectify** inaccurate data — you can edit identification details in the app.
- **Erase** your data — you can delete individual photos, or delete your entire
  account from the account menu at any time.
- **Export / portability** — request a copy of your data by emailing
  **l.siemanski@gmail.com**; we will provide your photos and records manually.
- **Object** to or restrict processing, and **lodge a complaint** with your local data
  protection supervisory authority.

## Cookies

We use only essential cookies required to keep you signed in. There are no advertising,
analytics, or tracking cookies, so no cookie consent banner is required.

## Children

Landmark Lore is not intended for anyone under 16. Please do not create an account if
you are under 16.

## AI accuracy

Identifications are generated by an AI model and may be incomplete or wrong. They are
provided for interest only and are not authoritative or professional advice.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the project evolves. The "Last updated" date above
reflects the latest version.
